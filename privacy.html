Privacy notice

SME Constellation · Making AI work for SMEs · 24 November 2026

This explains what happens to the information you add to the SME Constellation app at Malta Employers’ conference “Making AI work for SMEs – From policy to practice”, and your rights. Version 21 October 2026.

Who is responsible

Malta Employers’ Association, 35/1 South Street, Valletta VLT 1100, Malta, is the controller of your personal data in this app. The app is built and run on its behalf by TETRA (Kevin Spiteri), who acts as its processor.

Questions, or to use any of your rights: admin@maltaemployers.com.

What we collect

  • Your profile (public to attendees): name, organisation, and optionally your role, sector, whether you are a speaker, your reasons for attending and what you are working on.
  • Your contact card (private): optionally your mobile, email and LinkedIn or website.
  • Connections: who you ask to connect with, the short message you send, and who accepts.
  • Questions and votes: questions you send to speakers (your name only if you add it), your votes on questions and your poll answers.
  • Technical data: to stop misuse and to count how many phones opened the app, we keep a one-way scrambled code made from your network address and browser. We never keep your network address itself. Your phone also remembers your own profile, a random code used so you can only vote once, and which tab you were on. These are needed for the app to work and are not used to track you.

Why we use it, and on what legal basis

  • Showing your profile to other attendees, matching you with people to meet, and the AI features below: your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)), given with the first tick box.
  • Sharing your contact card with someone whose request you accept: your consent, given each time you accept.
  • The event report for Malta Employers: your consent, only if you tick the optional report box.
  • Emails about similar events: your consent, only if you tick the optional email box.
  • Questions, votes and polls: Malta Employers’ legitimate interest in running an interactive conference (Article 6(1)(f)). These are anonymous unless you add your name.
  • Preventing misuse (rate limits): legitimate interest in keeping the app secure.

Matches are worked out by simple rules from the reasons people tick, not by AI. No decision with legal or similarly significant effects is made about you by automated means.

Who can see what

  • Other attendees see your profile. Your name, sector and speaker status may appear on the big screen in the hall.
  • People whose request you accept see your contact card (not your mobile if you chose to hide it), and can save it to their phone.
  • Malta Employers receive an event report. It names only people who ticked the report box, with their profile, contact card (not a hidden mobile) and the people they connected with, where both people agreed. People who ticked only the email box appear with name, organisation and email. Everyone else is only counted in totals.
  • Service providers who act on our instructions: Hostinger (web hosting, where the app and its data are stored) and Anthropic (the AI features, see below).

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

How AI is used

Some features use Claude, an AI model by Anthropic. Text written by AI is labelled as such in the app.

  • Suggested opening line: when you tap it, your profile and the other person’s profile (name, organisation, role, sector, reasons, what you’re working on) are sent to Claude to suggest a conversation starter.
  • “Read the room” on the big screen: sector, role and reasons for attending are sent without names, to write a short summary of who is in the room.
  • Grouping audience questions for the moderator: the question texts are sent, without names.

Contact details are never sent to the AI. Under Anthropic’s commercial terms, Anthropic may not use this data to train its AI models. It processes the data only to provide the service, under a data processing agreement, and deletes it from its systems within 30 days (longer only if needed to investigate misuse or where the law requires).

Anthropic is based in the United States. Transfers are protected by the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses, which form part of Anthropic’s data processing agreement.

How long we keep it

The app and everything in it are deleted by 31 December 2026. You can remove your profile sooner at any time: open the app, go to “Add yourself” and tap “Remove me”. That deletes your profile, contact card and connections straight away. Contacts other people have already saved to their own phone are theirs to keep.

If you ticked the report or email box, Malta Employers keeps that information under its own data protection policy for as long as it needs it for those purposes, or until you withdraw your consent.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, receive a copy of or delete your data, object to or restrict how it is used, and withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect what was done before. You can edit or remove most things yourself in the app; for anything else, email admin@maltaemployers.com.

You can also complain to Malta’s data protection authority, the Information and Data Protection Commissioner.

Do you have to give your data?

No. The app is optional and you can attend the conference without it. To appear on the map you need a name, an organisation and at least one reason for attending; everything else is optional. Only share what you’re happy to share.

This notice may be updated before the event. The version you agreed to is recorded with your profile.
