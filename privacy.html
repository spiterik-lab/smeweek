Privacy notice

SME Constellation · Making AI work for SMEs · 24 November 2026

This explains what happens to the information you add to the SME Constellation app at Malta Employers’ conference “Making AI work for SMEs – From policy to practice”, and your rights. Version 15 November 2026 (app Version 2.0).

Who is responsible

Malta Employers’ Association, 35/1 South Street, Valletta VLT 1100, Malta, is the controller of your personal data in this app. The app is built and run on its behalf by TETRA (Kevin Spiteri), who acts as its processor.

Questions, or to use any of your rights: admin@maltaemployers.com.

What we collect

  • Your account (private): the email address you sign in with and your password, which is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form that cannot be read back.
  • Your profile (public to attendees): name, organisation, and optionally your role, sector, kind of organisation, where you are with AI, the areas where you want AI to work for you, the kinds of people you want to meet, whether you are a speaker, what you offer and are looking for, and what you are working on.
  • Your contact card (private): optionally your mobile, email and LinkedIn or website.
  • Requests and matches: who you ask to meet, the short message you send, who accepts, and which contact details each of you chose to share.
  • Messages: messages you exchange with a person you have matched with.
  • Notifications: if you switch them on, the address your browser gives us so that a notification can reach this device.
  • Missions and points: which parts of the app you have used (for example, completed your profile, made a match, asked a question), to work out your points and level.
  • Questions and votes: questions you send to speakers (your name only if you add it), your votes on questions and your poll answers.
  • Technical data: to stop misuse and to count how many phones opened the app, we keep a one-way scrambled code made from your network address and browser. We never keep your network address itself. Your phone also remembers that you are signed in, your own profile, a random code used so you can only vote once, and whether you have seen the opening screens. If you add the app to your home screen, its own files are kept on your phone so it opens quickly. These are needed for the app to work and are not used to track you.

Why we use it, and on what legal basis

  • Showing your profile to other attendees, matching you with people to meet, and the AI features below: your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)), given with the first tick box.
  • Your account, messages, notifications, missions and points: these are features you choose to use, and we use the data only to provide them (Article 6(1)(b), or your consent where you switch a feature on, such as notifications).
  • Sharing your contact card with someone you match with: your consent, given each time you ask or accept, for the details you choose. You can change it for each person afterwards.
  • The event report for Malta Employers: your consent, only if you tick the optional report box.
  • Emails about similar events: your consent, only if you tick the optional email box.
  • Questions, votes and polls: Malta Employers’ legitimate interest in running an interactive conference (Article 6(1)(f)). These are anonymous unless you add your name.
  • Preventing misuse (rate limits): legitimate interest in keeping the app secure.

Suggested matches are worked out by simple, published rules from what people tick (what they offer and need, their sector, kind of organisation, AI stage, focus areas and who they want to meet). If you type your own words (your role, what you’re working on, or your own sector), AI reads them once to work out which of those same tick-box options they fit, so that typed text can count too; the rules then do the matching. Each suggestion shows its reasons, and says so when a reason comes from a description. No decision with legal or similarly significant effects is made about you by automated means.

Who can see what

  • Other attendees see your profile. Your name, sector and speaker status may appear on the big screen in the hall.
  • People you match with (you both agreed to meet) see the parts of your contact card you chose to share with them (never a mobile you chose to hide), can save them to their phone, and can exchange messages with you. Only the two of you can read those messages in the app.
  • The leaderboard shows your name and points to other attendees. You can switch this off in the Me tab.
  • Malta Employers receive an event report. It names only people who ticked the report box, with their profile, contact card (not a hidden mobile) and the people they connected with, where both people agreed. People who ticked only the email box appear with name, organisation and email. Everyone else is only counted in totals.
  • Service providers who act on our instructions: Hostinger (web hosting, where the app and its data are stored, and which sends password reset emails) and Anthropic (the AI features, see below).
  • Notification services: if you switch on notifications, they are delivered through the service built into your browser or phone (run by Google, Apple, Mozilla or Microsoft). The content is encrypted so that only your device can read it.
  • Google Fonts: the app’s lettering (fonts) is loaded from Google Fonts, which sees your network address when the page loads.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

How AI is used

Some features use Claude, an AI model by Anthropic. Text written by AI is labelled as such in the app.

  • Suggested opening line: when you tap it, your profile and the other person’s profile (name, organisation, role, sector, kind of organisation, AI stage, focus areas, what you each offer and look for, and what you’re working on) are sent to Claude to suggest a conversation starter.
  • Reading what you typed, for matching: when you save your profile, the words you typed for your role, what you’re working on and your own sector, with your organisation’s name, are sent to Claude to work out which of the app’s tick-box options they fit. Your name and contact details are not sent.
  • “Read the room” on the big screen: sector, kind of organisation, role, AI stage and what people offer and look for are sent without names, to write a short summary of who is in the room.
  • Grouping audience questions for the moderator: the question texts are sent, without names.

Contact details, sign-in details and messages are never sent to the AI. Under Anthropic’s commercial terms, Anthropic may not use this data to train its AI models. It processes the data only to provide the service, under a data processing agreement, and deletes it from its systems within 30 days (longer only if needed to investigate misuse or where the law requires).

Anthropic is based in the United States. Transfers are protected by the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses, which form part of Anthropic’s data processing agreement.

How long we keep it

The app and everything in it are deleted by 31 December 2026. You can remove your profile sooner at any time: open the app, go to the “Me” tab and tap “Delete my account”. That deletes your account, profile, contact card, requests, matches, messages and notification settings straight away. Contacts other people have already saved to their own phone are theirs to keep.

If you ticked the report or email box, Malta Employers keeps that information under its own data protection policy for as long as it needs it for those purposes, or until you withdraw your consent.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, receive a copy of or delete your data, object to or restrict how it is used, and withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect what was done before. You can edit or remove most things yourself in the app; for anything else, email admin@maltaemployers.com.

You can also complain to Malta’s data protection authority, the Information and Data Protection Commissioner.

Do you have to give your data?

No. The app is optional and you can attend the conference without it. To use it you need an account (an email address and password), a name, an organisation and at least one thing you offer or are looking for; everything else is optional. Only share what you’re happy to share.

This notice may be updated before the event. The version you agreed to is recorded with your profile.
